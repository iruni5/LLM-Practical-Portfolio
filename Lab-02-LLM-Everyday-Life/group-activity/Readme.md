# Lab 02 – Group Activity: PackWise

## Overview

PackWise is a student-focused application developed as part of the Lab 02 group activity, *The Forgetful Student*.

The application aims to help university students remember and manage the items they need throughout their academic day by incorporating daily checklists, schedule-based preparation, and AI-powered assistance.

## The Problem

University students often forget essential items when moving between lectures, laboratories, and other academic activities.

PackWise addresses this challenge by helping students organize their daily requirements and prepare for upcoming activities.

## Product Features

- Daily checklist.
- Schedule management.
- Item prioritization.
- Forgetting probability indicators.
- AI-powered assistant.
- Scan interface.
- Progress tracking.
- Leaving simulation.
