# PackWise – Group Activity

## 1. Problem Statement

University students often move between lectures, laboratories, and other academic activities throughout the day. During busy schedules, they may forget essential items such as student identification cards, laptops, chargers, USB drives, textbooks, and assignment materials.

Managing these daily requirements can become challenging, particularly when students have different activities scheduled throughout the day.

## 2. Product Concept

PackWise is a student-focused application designed to help students remember and manage the items they need for their daily university activities.

The product provides a centralized interface for managing daily packing checklists and academic schedules while incorporating AI-powered assistance to support student preparation.

## 3. Product Name

**PackWise**

The name represents a smarter approach to preparing and organizing the items students need throughout their academic day.

## 4. Target Users

The primary target users are university students who regularly attend lectures, laboratories, workshops, and other academic activities.

The application is designed to help students organize their daily requirements and reduce the likelihood of forgetting essential belongings.

## 5. Key Features

- Daily packing checklist.
- Schedule-based preparation.
- Item prioritization.
- Forgetting probability indicators.
- AI-powered assistant.
- Scan interface.
- Daily progress tracking.
- Leaving simulation.

## 6. Application of Large Language Models

The group activity explores the practical application of Large Language Models in an everyday student challenge.

An AI-powered assistant is incorporated into PackWise to provide contextual assistance related to student preparation and daily activities.

The project demonstrates how LLMs can be incorporated into a practical application to support productivity and problem-solving.

## 7. Visual Prototype

PackWise was developed as a web-based application with a dashboard and navigation interface for accessing its features.

Screenshots of the application's interface and features are available in the `screenshots/` directory.
