# Lab 02 – LLMs in Everyday Life

## Objective

To explore how Large Language Models can be applied to everyday problems and understand their practical applications in real-world situations.

The laboratory encourages critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity, and the development of AI-powered product concepts.

## Activities

This laboratory involved exploring the applications of Large Language Models in everyday life through practical activities and a group-based product development task.

### Group Activity – The Forgetful Student

The group activity focused on addressing a common challenge faced by university students: forgetting essential items required for their daily academic activities.

A product concept called **PackWise** was developed to help students manage their daily packing requirements through checklists, academic schedules, and AI-powered assistance.

## Learning Outcomes

- Explored real-world applications of Large Language Models.
- Identified everyday problems that could be addressed using AI.
- Developed a product concept based on a real-world challenge.
- Explored the role of LLMs in AI-powered applications.
- Gained exposure to collaborative product development.
- Explored the development of a visual application prototype.
